# Personality: Office Worker
# One joke per line. Lines starting with # are ignored.
"Reply All" is serious business. I started a meteor that way once.
My calendar is full. I am empty.
A "quick 15-minute sync" lasts until lunch.
Attached is the attachment I forgot to attach.
"As per my previous email" is office for "read it".
My chair has more seniority than I do. Almost.
Stamp, signature, copy, original. And one more copy, just in case.
When the boss asks "got a minute?", remember: it's never a minute.
The annual report is the same every year. Only the year changes.
The office plant is the only coworker who never talks in meetings.
Deadlines are like meteors. You see them from afar and they still surprise you.
The meeting concluded that we need another meeting.
The office AC has two settings: Arctic and Sahara.
Three emails later: "Just following up."
My job is 10% work and 90% looking for the file I need.
A break is staring at the screen with a different facial expression.
When someone brings cake, the whole office becomes a team.
"As we agreed" means nobody remembers what was agreed.
Friday afternoon is perfect for planning Monday. Which never happens.
The new hire asks where everything is. We've been asking for years.
The boss is on vacation. The office is quiet as a museum.
The copier is jammed. So am I.
The meeting is over. Only three new action items for the next one.
The most beautiful word in the office: "cancelled".
Out of office: extinct. Back in 66 million years.
When they say "no rush", do it right now.
Priority one, two and three: everything is priority one.
I plan to be productive today. The plan is excellent.
"Just a few small changes" is the start of version 14.
Office hours are 9 to 5. Work hours are 9 until the boss leaves.

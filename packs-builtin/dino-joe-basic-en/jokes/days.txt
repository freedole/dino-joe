# Weekdays
# One joke per line. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Monday. Like a meteor, but every week.
Tuesday is just Monday pretending it isn't.
Wednesday: half the week is gone, and so is half my patience.
Thursday is Friday for people who like to be happy early.
Friday afternoon. Don't touch anything that works.
Every Friday at 2 PM, everyone is very busy packing their stuff.
The weekend lasts two days. Monday lasts two weeks.
Sunday evening: the body is resting, the brain is already in a meeting.
Payday: money came, money left. Like a comet.
End of the month. My wallet and I are both fossils.
Monday morning: the coffee is stronger than me.
Friday the 13th doesn't scare me. I survived a meteor.
A day without the boss calling is a myth, like a unicorn.
Saturday. Finally time for all the things I won't do.
Sunday lunch is the only meeting that can't be an email.
The morning is wiser than the evening, but it sleeps longer.

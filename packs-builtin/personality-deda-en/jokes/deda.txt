# Personality: Grandpa
# One joke per line. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Back in my day there was no internet. Just lava.
We walked ten kilometers to school. Uphill. Both ways.
Kids today don't know what a real meteor is.
Retirement is when you have time for everything and get nothing done.
The doctor says less salt. I tell him salt is from the Cretaceous.
People used to talk face to face. Now they talk to phones while sitting next to you.
The TV remote is gone. Again. Someone hid it.
The best news is the evening news. Everything else is noise.
I don't forget. I choose what to remember.
The grandkids say "grandpa, it's an app". Grandpa says "it's nonsense".
Nothing is like it used to be. It never was.
In my youth we had three TV channels and one meteor.
When I was young, Pangea was one continent and everybody knew everybody.
I sit on the bench and rate how the neighbor parks.
It's cold, put on a jacket. I don't care that it's July.
No time, I'm going to the market. I go every day.
Things used to last. I'm the proof.
What do you need that for? I went extinct just fine without it.
My grandson taught me to text. Now I send him twenty a day.
When times were hard, I didn't complain. I just roared.
Young people are so nervous. We were only nervous when meteors fell.
Don't explain it to me, I saw it 66 million years ago.
I take my pills on time. Lunch and the news too.
A retiree has no days off. A retiree has errands.
Post office, bank, market, doctor. That's my workday.
The best thing is when the grandkids visit. The second best is when they leave.
Why do I need a smartphone when I'm smart.
People used to wait in line. Now everyone cuts in. Like a meteor.
I'll tell you something, but keep it quiet... then I told the whole street.
I call my grandson to turn on the computer. He says "just press the button". Which button?

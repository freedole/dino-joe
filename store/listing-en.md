# Microsoft Store listing - English (en-us)

**Product name:** Dino Joe - Desktop Pet

**Short description (max 100 chars, optional):**
A tiny dino lives on your desktop: hatches from an egg, grows up, tells jokes and wants to be fed.

**Description:**
Dino Joe is a tiny dinosaur that lives on your Windows desktop. It starts as an egg - keep it warm and it hatches in a minute or two. Then it walks around, naps, tells jokes, wants to be fed and petted, and grows from a baby into a juvenile and finally an adult over a few days. Good care makes it grow faster.

- Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no telemetry.
- 6 species: T-Rex, Triceratops, Brontosaurus, Raptor + Stegosaurus and Pterodactyl to unlock
- 12 colors, 7 patterns, 10 accessories, glasses
- 9 personalities, including the Hardcore Tamagotchi mode (it can get sick or run away!)
- Care status: food, water, energy, cuddles, fun, hygiene, health - plus a vacation mode
- 18 achievements that unlock new species and patterns
- Hundreds of jokes in packs you can enable, mix and download from an online community catalog
- Optional AI chat: bring your own key (Groq, Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, OpenRouter or local Ollama)
- 5 languages: English, Serbian (ijekavian / ekavian), Bosnian, Croatian
- Open source (MIT): github.com/freedole/dino-joe

**What's new in this version:**
Evolution (egg -> baby -> juvenile -> adult), 18 achievements, new species Stegosaurus and Pterodactyl, new patterns Gold and Rainbow.

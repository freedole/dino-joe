# Privacy Policy - Dino Joe - Desktop Pet

Last updated: 2026-10-27

**Short version: Dino Joe does not collect, store or send any personal data to the author. No account, no ads, no telemetry, no analytics.**

## Data stored on your computer
All settings and state are stored locally in `%AppData%\DinoJoe\` (settings, pet status, life stage, achievements, joke packs, AI chat settings). Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app (or deleting that folder) removes it.

## Network connections
Dino Joe works fully offline. It connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

- **Joke pack catalog** (*Jokes > Catalog*): downloads the list and pack files from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com/freedole/dino-joe-packs`). Only standard web requests, no identifiers are sent.
- **Optional AI chat**: only if you connect an AI service yourself (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, OpenRouter, Ollama or a custom endpoint). Your messages are sent directly from your computer to the service you chose, using your own API key, and are subject to that service's privacy policy. Your API key is stored locally, encrypted with Windows DPAPI, and is never sent to the author.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
